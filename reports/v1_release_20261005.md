## Remaining acceptance / limits

- Real-browser video selection, moving playback with changing inference/heatmap,
  stop-backend/recovery without reload, downloaded CSV/JSON and 15-minute browser
  soak still require manual verification due to automation blockage. The backend
  soak does not prove browser rendering/smoothness or client memory behavior.
- One shared session, no authentication/session isolation. Keep loopback only.
  Multipart upload limit is after parsing; no duration/conversion/disk quota or
  hardened media sandbox. Do not expose to LAN/internet or tunnel publicly.
- GPU/CPU performance varies; CPU may be too slow. Lossy playback conversion and
  JPEG browser samples are not pixel-identical to offline inference. Full-scene
  resize changes image scale and potentially count error. Heatmap is not people/m².
- Prototype attention thresholds are not validated safety limits. Mall/short HT21
  do not establish target-camera festival reliability; no correction multiplier.
- Forced process kill can leave owned temporary copies. No artifact license or
  media permission is invented. Fresh checkout requires trusted model separately.
- Git remote remains unset; no remote was invented or pushed. Local source-only
  backup excludes binaries/footage/results notebook embedded imagery. Prior
  unrelated main.py deletion and historical local reports remain untouched.

Next stage: human-reviewed target-camera empty/night/dense-scene count evaluation,
measure error/bias before scene-specific thresholds. Network hosting additionally
requires authentication, session ownership, pre-parsing upload limits, bounded
duration/conversion/storage/compute. Audience, hardware and budget must be defined
before choosing hosting; no new milestone or training was started here.
